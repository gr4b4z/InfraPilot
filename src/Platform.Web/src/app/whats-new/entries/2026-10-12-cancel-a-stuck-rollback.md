---
title: Cancel a stuck rollback
date: 2026-10-12
audience: admins
link: /rollbacks
linkLabel: Rollbacks
---
Admins can now cancel a rollback that's approved or in progress, or skip one of its services, giving a reason that's shown on the rollback card. Rollbacks also close by themselves when the environment has already moved on to the target version or a newer one.
