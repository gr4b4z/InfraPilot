The **Mass approve** button on the Promotions page lets you sign one approval step — say *QA Review* into production — on every promotion waiting for it, in one go. Pick the environment and the step, check the list (promotions you can't sign are greyed out with the reason), and note the warning for any that deploy as soon as you confirm. Afterwards you see the result for each promotion. Admins can bypass from the same dialog.
