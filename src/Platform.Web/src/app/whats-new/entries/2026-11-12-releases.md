The new **Releases** page follows a release such as 5.10 step by step, from planning and the version
cut through staging, go/no-go and release day to the sanity test in production. The release manager
moves each step on, and the portal shows how many of the release's services are on staging, approved,
deployed to production and migrated, and names the ones that still need someone. Each step links
the pipeline runs behind it, and the release keeps its Jira version, release plan and other pages
in one place.

Everyone can follow a release. Release managers and admins open one with **New release** and move
its steps on. A product's release managers are the people who give the **Release Manager** approval
on its promotions to production.
