The new **Releases** page follows a release such as 5.10 step by step, from planning and the version
cut through staging, go/no-go and release day to the sanity test in production. The release manager
moves each step on, and the portal shows how many of the release's services are on staging, approved,
deployed to production and migrated. Steps the portal can check show the release's services in a table
(builds, staging deploys, QA Review, approvals, deployments, migrations) that you can sort by any
column, and the release keeps its Jira version, release plan and other pages in one place.

Everyone can follow a release. Release managers and admins open one with **New release**: a version,
its products and the release date. Every service of those products is in the release; untick the ones
that don't ship from the **Services** tile. A product's release managers are the people who give the
**Release Manager** approval on its promotions to production.
