---
title: Smoother updates, no more blank screens
date: 2026-10-09
audience: everyone
---
When a new version of the portal is released while you have it open, a banner lets you reload — and it switches over by itself the next time you go to another page. If a page ever fails to load, you'll see what went wrong and a **Copy details** button to paste into your bug report, instead of a blank screen.
