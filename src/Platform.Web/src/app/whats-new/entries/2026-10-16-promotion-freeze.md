Admins can now freeze a product's promotions into an environment — during a release window or an incident, for example. While the freeze is on, approvals and sign-offs are still recorded, but nothing is approved to go out. When it's lifted, everything that was waiting is checked again and moves on.

A blue banner and a **Frozen** group on the Promotions page show what's being held. On the Deployments pages a snowflake marks frozen environments, and a **During freeze** badge flags any deploy that landed while a freeze was on. Admins set freezes up in **Settings → Freeze**.
