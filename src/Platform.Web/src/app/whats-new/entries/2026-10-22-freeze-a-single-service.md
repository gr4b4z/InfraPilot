---
title: Freeze promotions for a single service
date: 2026-10-22
audience: admins
link: /settings/freeze
linkLabel: Settings → Freeze
---
A Promotion Freeze no longer has to cover a whole product. In **Settings → Freeze**, pick one service to hold only its promotions while the rest of the product keeps moving. The banners on the Promotions and Deployments pages name the frozen service.
