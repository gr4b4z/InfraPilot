title: Your QA team is always one click away when assigning
date: 2026-11-20
audience: qa
link: /me/work-items
linkLabel: Work items
---
Admins can now list the people who usually take a role, such as your QA team for **QA owner**, under
**Settings → Participant Roles → Team**. When you assign that role, the picker lists the team first,
before you type anything.

The **Assigned to** filter on **Work items** now always lists the team as well, even people with
nothing assigned right now. Anyone else holding a required role is listed below them.
