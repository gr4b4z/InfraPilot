title: Deployments show pending and failed migrations
date: 2026-11-02
audience: everyone
link: /deployments
linkLabel: Deployments
---
When a deployment's migration is still pending or has failed, a small database chip says so on the product's deployment matrix, in its activity feed and in the service's history. Click the chip to open the migration. A deployment's own page now has a **Migrations** card that links to its schema and data migrations.
