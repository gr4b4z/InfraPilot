title: Record production incidents for the delivery metrics
date: 2026-10-26
audience: admins
link: /incidents
linkLabel: Incidents
---
A new **Incidents** page under **System** lets you record a production incident: which service and environment, when it started, when service was restored, and a link to the incident in your own tooling. On a deployment's page, **Record incident** pre-fills the service, environment and version, which ties the incident to the deployment that caused it.

Recorded incidents feed **Change failure rate** and **Time to restore service** on the **Delivery metrics** report. Only the figures are shown there; the records stay on this page.
