The new **Agent access** page, in your user menu, lets you create a personal access token and copy ready-made setup for assistants such as Claude Code or VS Code. Once connected, your assistant can look up deployments, promotions, work items and builds for you, and explain why a promotion or deployment is stuck. A standard token can't approve, reject or sign off anything.
