---
title: A clear message when people search is down
date: 2026-10-19
audience: everyone
---
If the company directory can't be reached, the person pickers — assigning work items, choosing approvers — now say **Directory search is unavailable right now** instead of showing no matches, so you know it isn't a typo or a missing person. Admins also see a banner at the top of the portal until it's fixed.
