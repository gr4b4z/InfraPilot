The **Delivery metrics** report now has a **Trends** section: each DORA metric as a full chart over the selected window, with the previous period drawn as a dashed line underneath for comparison. Tick **per product** to overlay each product's own line and see who moves the overall one, and use **Add metric…** to chart any other figure on the page. Points resting on thin data are drawn hollow so a blip isn't mistaken for a change.
