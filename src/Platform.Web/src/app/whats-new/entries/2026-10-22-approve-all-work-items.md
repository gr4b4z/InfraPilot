title: Approve all work items on a promotion at once
date: 2026-10-22
audience: qa
link: /promotions
linkLabel: Promotions
---
The **Work items** card on a promotion has a new **Approve all** button. Before you confirm, it lists every work item still waiting for a decision — those are the only ones signed off. Items that already have an approval, an issue or a block are left as they are.
