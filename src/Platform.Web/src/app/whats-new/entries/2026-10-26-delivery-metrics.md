A new **Delivery metrics** page, next to **Analytics**, reports deployment frequency, lead time, deploy failure and rollback rates, stories shipped, approval and deploy latency and story coverage over the products you pick — all of them, or any subset — for a fixed window such as **Last month** or **Last quarter**. Every figure says how it was counted and how much of the data it could see, and the page breaks the numbers down per product and per service. Export it as CSV for a report.
