date: 2026-11-20

A service's page now has a **Service inventory** section: the team that owns it, where it runs, its databases and resources, and what it cost this month. **Service cost** shows the same per product or team, month by month, and **Cost coverage** shows how much of the cloud cost can be traced to a service at all.

The information is read from the service catalog and is shown with its age. It appears once an admin turns on **ServiceInventory** in **Settings → Feature Flags**.
