title: Assign one person to every work item
date: 2026-10-07
audience: qa

---
The **Work items** card on a promotion has an **Assign to all** button: put one person into a role, such as QA owner, on every work item at once — or only on those that don't have anyone yet. The person picker for a role now suggests only people from the groups set up for that role.
