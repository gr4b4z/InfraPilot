---
title: A bypass no longer signs off work items for you
date: 2026-10-22
audience: qa
---
When an admin bypasses a promotion's approval gate, its open work items are now marked **Bypassed** instead of Approved. The same work items on other promotions still wait for a real decision, and your own sign-off replaces the Bypassed mark whenever you give it. If the bypass is cancelled, the marks are removed again.
