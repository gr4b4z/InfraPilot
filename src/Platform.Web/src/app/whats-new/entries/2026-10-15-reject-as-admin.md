title: Admins can reject any promotion
date: 2026-10-15
audience: admins

A promotion page now offers **Reject as admin** when you aren't one of its approvers, so stale or unwanted promotions can be cleared without waiting for the people named on the gate. A reason is required, and the audit shows the rejection as an admin decision.
