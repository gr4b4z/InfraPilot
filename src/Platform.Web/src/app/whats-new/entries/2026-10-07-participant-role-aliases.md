title: Participant roles understand Jira's names
date: 2026-10-07
audience: admins
link: /settings/roles
linkLabel: Settings → Participant Roles
---
In **Settings → Participant Roles**, each role can list the other names it arrives under (**Also sent as**), so a ticket whose Jira "QA" field is filled in no longer reads "Needs QA owner". You can also choose the directory groups people are picked from for each role.
